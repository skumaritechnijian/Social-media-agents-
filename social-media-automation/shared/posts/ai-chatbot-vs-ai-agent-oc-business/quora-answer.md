# Quora Answer: What is the difference between an AI chatbot and an AI agent?

An AI chatbot and an AI agent are related, but they are not the same thing.

An AI chatbot is mainly designed for conversation. It answers questions, guides users through simple flows, captures leads, routes support requests, and helps people find information. A chatbot is useful when the goal is communication.

An AI agent is more action-oriented. It can use tools, connect to business systems, follow multi-step workflows, and complete tasks with the right permissions. For example, an AI agent might check a CRM, summarize a ticket, draft a follow-up email, schedule a meeting, or update a record.

The simple difference is:

- Chatbot = answers questions
- AI agent = takes action

For businesses, the right choice depends on the workflow.

Use a chatbot when you need:

- Website FAQs
- Lead capture
- Basic customer support routing
- Appointment guidance
- Simple customer conversations

Use an AI agent when you need:

- Workflow automation
- CRM, helpdesk, calendar, or document integration
- Internal productivity support
- Multi-step task execution
- Human approval before final action

The biggest risk with AI agents is governance. Because agents can act inside business systems, companies need strong controls around permissions, data access, audit logs, and approval workflows.

For most businesses, the best approach is to start with a narrow use case, measure the value, and expand carefully.
