# Quora Answer: What should be included in a technical SEO audit for a local business website?

A good technical SEO audit for a local business website should focus on whether Google can crawl, index, understand, and trust the site. For Orange County businesses, this matters because local rankings depend on more than just keywords and Google Business Profile optimization.

The most important technical SEO audit areas are:

1. Crawlability and indexing  
Check Google Search Console to confirm that important service pages are indexed and that low-value pages are not wasting crawl attention.

2. XML sitemap and robots.txt  
Make sure the sitemap includes the pages you actually want Google to discover, and confirm that robots.txt is not blocking important resources or sections.

3. Site speed and Core Web Vitals  
Review mobile speed, layout shift, image size, caching, JavaScript performance, and hosting quality.

4. Broken links and redirects  
Fix 404 errors, redirect chains, old URLs, and broken internal links. These create poor user experience and dilute SEO value.

5. Duplicate content and canonical tags  
Duplicate titles, duplicate meta descriptions, and incorrect canonicals can confuse search engines about which page should rank.

6. Structured data  
Use schema markup where appropriate, including Organization, LocalBusiness, Breadcrumb, Article, FAQ, and Service schema.

7. Internal linking  
Your blog posts and service pages should support each other. Internal links help Google understand which pages are most important.

8. Security and HTTPS  
HTTPS, clean redirects, and secure site behavior are basic trust signals.

For local businesses, technical SEO should connect directly to lead generation. The audit should not stop at reporting errors. It should prioritize fixes that improve rankings, conversions, and local visibility.
