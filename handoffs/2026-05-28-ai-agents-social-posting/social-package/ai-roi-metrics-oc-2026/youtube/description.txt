AI ROI is not about chasing novelty. For Orange County businesses, the useful metrics are practical and measurable: time saved, fewer manual handoffs, faster response times, better lead quality, stronger customer follow-up, and lower operational drag.

In this video, Technijian explains how to evaluate AI automation by business outcomes instead of tool hype.

Key points:
- Measure time saved in repeated workflows
- Track response speed and handoff reduction
- Watch lead quality and follow-up consistency
- Connect AI projects to operating cost and revenue impact
- Avoid AI initiatives that do not improve a measurable process
